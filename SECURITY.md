# Security

If you think you've found a security problem in Teleplay, please **don't open a public issue**. Email **info@teleplay.ai** instead (English or Turkish), with the steps to reproduce it and what you think the impact is.

Please:

- test only with your own account and your own games;
- don't read, change or delete anyone else's data;
- don't run tests that slow down or take down the service;
- give us a reasonable time to fix it before you talk about it publicly.

Our contact details are also in [security.txt](https://www.teleplay.ai/.well-known/security.txt).
